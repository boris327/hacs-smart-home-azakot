Do & don’t

Do

  • Keep #ff6105 for the primary CTA, the active step badge, and the accent rule.
  • Set display headlines at 48–78px in weight 300 with -0.030em to -0.036em tracking.
  • Stay on the radius ladder: 5 / 8 / 24 / 32 / 50.
  • Stack surfaces in order: white → linen → bone → peach → charcoal.
  • Use hairline 1px borders as the primary structural device.

Don’t

  • Don’t add a second accent hue — the system is monochrome plus one orange.
  • Don’t use weight 600 or 700; the scale tops out at 500.
  • Don’t put shadows on cards, images, or secondary buttons.
  • Don’t set display text in all-caps or with positive letter-spacing.
  • Don’t fill large areas with orange — it punctuates, it doesn’t paint.
